# Data acquisition

The artifact does not commit third-party raw datasets. Obtain the exact source
records listed below, create a local data root, and preserve the expected base
filenames in `checksums.tsv`.

- AUS-Wheat: https://doi.org/10.25909/5becfa45c176f
- Ward wheat: https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.7700012.v1
- GABI genotype: https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.n02v6wwzc
- GABI phenotype: https://doi.org/10.5447/ipk/2022/18
- EasyGeSe processed datasets: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15348871

The three wheat checksums identify the exact analysis-ready files used in the
experiments. The EasyGeSe checksums identify the exact X/Y/Z files. Upstream
archives may require processing before their derived checksum matches; those
processing entry points will be connected during Stage A/B.
